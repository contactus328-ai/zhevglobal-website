Why this matters

An experiential immersion programme offering real-world global exposure

GUIDE helps students step beyond the classroom and experience university environments, real-world industries and Singapore's vibrant setting. Curated visits and guided reflection help students broaden their perspective and make more informed decisions about academic interests and future pathways.

  • Explore academic disciplines and university environments.
  • Connect classroom learning with real-world industry and sustainability.
  • Build cultural awareness through international exposure.
  • Reflect on strengths, interests and possible career directions.

Need to know

GUIDE programme FAQs

Answers to common questions about eligibility, schedule, visits and programme arrangements.

GUIDE stands for Global University, Industry & Career Discovery Experience. It is a residential Singapore immersion programme combining university exposure, industry visits and cultural exploration.
The programme page states that it is designed for school students in Grades 6–12. Confirm the grade range and any cohort-specific age requirements with the organiser before registering.
The webpage labels the programme as six days and displays dates of 20–26 December 2026. Confirm the exact departure and return dates because the displayed date range and duration may include travel days differently.
The page highlights the National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU). Specific access and sessions are subject to the confirmed itinerary.
The webpage lists the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA), Marina Barrage and industry insights as part of the programme experience.
Listed inclusions are career sessions, university and industry visits, twin-sharing accommodation, two meals per day, field trips, visa, insurance, local and airport transfers, city tour, Universal Studios entry and a flight fare capped at INR 30,000. Obtain the final written inclusions and exclusions list.
The public webpage does not provide a complete exclusions list. Ask the organiser to confirm any excess airfare above the stated cap, personal expenses, additional meals, optional activities and any other costs.
The page lists a Certificate of Participation. Confirm the issuing organisation and any attendance or completion requirements.
